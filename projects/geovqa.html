Ask the image

A vision-language model fine-tuned on satellite imagery answers questions about a scene and draws boxes around the evidence it used.

Demo with pre-computed answers over Nairobi. Swap in your model's outputs or API.

Approach

A CLIP-style vision encoder pre-trained on satellite image–caption pairs feeds a language model through a projection layer. The model outputs text plus bounding boxes in image coordinates, which are converted to map coordinates for display.
