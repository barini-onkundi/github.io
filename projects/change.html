What changed?

Drag the slider to compare two years of Sentinel-2 imagery, then ask the model to write a change report: what changed, where, and how much.

Real Sentinel-2 cloudless mosaics. Reports and figures are demo outputs.
2018
2023
Change report

Approach

A Siamese encoder embeds the two dates. A change head produces a pixel-level change mask, and a language decoder turns the mask and both embeddings into a short report. Area figures come from the mask, not from the language model, so the numbers can be checked.
